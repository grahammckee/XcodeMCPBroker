# Code of Conduct

Be thoughtful. Be direct. Do good work and treat people well.

AI tools are welcome. Vibecoding is not. If you submit something, you own it: understand it, test it, and be ready to explain the decisions and tradeoffs.

Disagreement is fine. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, spam, and sharing someone's private information are not.

Keep it honest, useful, and focused on the work.

Feel free to reach out to [contact@grahammckee.com](mailto:contact@grahammckee.com) for anything else.
